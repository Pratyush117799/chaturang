About Chaturanga

The ancient Indian war game that shaped the chess world — brought back to life for the modern age.

Origin & History

The Game That Started It All

Chaturanga — meaning "four divisions of the army" in Sanskrit — is the 6th-century CE Indian board game widely recognized as the ancestor of all modern chess variants, including Chess, Xiangqi (Chinese Chess), Shogi (Japanese Chess), and Janggi (Korean Chess).

Played on an 8×8 grid called the Ashtāpada, Chaturanga replicated the four branches of the ancient Indian military: the Gaja (elephant), the Ashva (horse/cavalry), the Ratha (chariot), and the Pada (foot soldier/infantry). Each piece moved differently, and crucially — movement was governed by the roll of a Pasha (dice), introducing probability as a core strategic element absent from modern chess.

The earliest written references to Chaturanga appear in the Harshacharita (c. 625 CE) and later Sanskrit texts. Scholars trace its spread westward through Persia as Chatrang and then Shatranj, eventually evolving into modern Chess as it reached medieval Europe. Eastward, it gave rise to the Chinese and Japanese variants still widely played today.

The Chaturanga project exists to ensure this foundational game is not merely a footnote in chess history — but a living, playable experience with its authentic rules, cultural context, and strategic depth fully intact.

Our Mission

Document and faithfully implement the authentic rules of Chaturanga — including dice-based movement, historical piece abilities, and traditional win conditions — so future generations can experience the game as it was played.

Features

What Makes This Different

Authentic Dice-Based Gameplay (Pasha)

Unlike modern chess, Chaturanga uses a Pasha (dice) to determine which piece type may move each turn. This probabilistic mechanic is historically attested and fundamentally changes strategy — every move is a calculated gamble.

Deep Cultural Context

Sanskrit piece names, historical Itihasa records, and the Acharya lesson system embed authentic Indian cultural context into every aspect of the game.

Seer Analysis Engine

Our built-in Seer engine evaluates board positions and suggests optimal moves — an AI coach adapted specifically for Chaturanga's unique probabilistic movement rules.

Real-Time Multiplayer & ELO

WebSocket-powered online multiplayer with room codes, reconnection handling, and a full ELO rating system so your rank reflects genuine Chaturanga skill.

Akhara Training Mode

Practice specific positions, drill piece movements, and sharpen your understanding of probabilistic strategy in a structured training environment.

Puzzles & Campaigns

Curated tactical puzzles and the Panchanga campaign mode offer structured challenges that deepen your understanding of Chaturanga's strategic landscape.
