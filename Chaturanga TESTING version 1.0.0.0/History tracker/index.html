After Shatranj

The tree forks three ways

By the time Shatranj was mature, chess wasn't one lineage anymore. India kept mutating its own game in parallel; separate variants were taking shape in East and Southeast Asia; and Europe was about to rewrite the rulebook entirely. This chronicle treats all three as real, distinct branches — not one tidy inevitable line.
