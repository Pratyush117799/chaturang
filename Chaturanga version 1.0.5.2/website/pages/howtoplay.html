    Cast the Pāśaka

    One rule ties every lesson above together: before each move, you roll the dice — and the number tells you which piece you're even allowed to touch.

    1
    Ratha only
    2
    Any piece, free choice
    3
    Ashwa only
    4
    Danti only
    5
    Any piece, free choice
    6
    Nara or Raja

    Break both enemy thrones

    A team wins once neither opposing Raja remains on the board and can no longer respawn. Keep your own Raja and Ratha close to allies — as you just saw, minor pieces can threaten the square, but never take it.
