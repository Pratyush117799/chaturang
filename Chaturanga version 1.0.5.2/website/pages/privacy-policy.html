1. Introduction

Welcome to Chaturanga ("we," "us," or "our"). This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, and protect information when you visit our website and use our services, including the Chaturanga online game, lessons, and multiplayer features.

By using our website, you agree to the practices described in this policy. If you do not agree, please do not use our services.

Contact: For any privacy questions, reach us at chaturangagame@gmail.com.

2. Information We Collect

Information You Provide

  • Account information: If you create an account, we collect your chosen username and email address.
  • Contact form submissions: Name, email address, and message content when you contact us.

Information Collected Automatically

  • Gameplay and progress data: Your game history, ELO rating, lesson progress, and puzzle completion status — stored to personalize your experience.
  • Usage data: Pages visited, time on site, referrer URLs, and browser/device type — collected via Google Analytics.
  • Cookies and similar technologies: Session cookies, persistent cookies, and advertising cookies placed by Google AdSense. See Section 6 for details.
  • IP address: Collected automatically by our server and analytics tools; used for security and geographic analytics only.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide, operate, and maintain the Chaturanga game and website.
  • To save and display your gameplay progress, ELO rating, and lesson history.
  • To personalize your experience (e.g., remembering your preferences).
  • To improve our features and fix bugs using aggregated analytics data.
  • To respond to your contact form messages and support requests.
  • To display relevant advertisements via Google AdSense (see Section 4).
  • To maintain the security of our services and prevent abuse.

We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

4. Google AdSense & Third-Party Advertising

We use Google AdSense to display advertisements on our website. Google AdSense is operated by Google LLC, a third-party vendor. Google uses cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to our website and other websites on the internet.

Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visit to our site and/or other sites on the Internet. You may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google's Ads Settings.

For more information on how Google uses data when you use our partners' sites or apps, please visit: policies.google.com/technologies/ads.

Cookie Consent

For visitors from the European Union, European Economic Area, or the United Kingdom, we display a cookie consent banner before any personalized-advertising cookies are set. You may choose to accept or decline personalized ads. Declining personalized ads means you may still see ads, but they will not be tailored to your interests.

Children and Advertising

If we determine that a significant portion of our audience consists of children under 13, we will configure our advertising to use non-personalized ads only and comply with Google's Families policy. See Section 7 for our children's privacy policy.

5. Google Analytics

We use Google Analytics to understand how visitors interact with our website. Google Analytics collects data such as page views, session duration, traffic source, and device type. This data is aggregated and anonymized — we cannot identify individual users from Analytics reports.

Google Analytics uses cookies (see Section 6). You can opt out of Google Analytics tracking by installing the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on.

6. Cookies

We use the following types of cookies:

  • Session cookies: Temporary cookies that expire when you close your browser. Used to maintain your login state and game session.
  • Persistent cookies: Remain on your device for a set period. Used to remember your preferences and ELO/progress data.
  • Analytics cookies: Placed by Google Analytics to collect usage statistics.
  • Advertising cookies: Placed by Google AdSense to serve personalized ads (subject to your consent where required by law).

Managing Cookies

You can manage or delete cookies through your browser settings. Note that disabling cookies may affect site functionality — for example, you may not be able to stay logged in or save game progress. Most browsers allow you to block third-party cookies specifically, which will disable personalized advertising without affecting the game itself.

7. Children's Privacy

Chaturanga is intended for users 13 years of age and older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has provided us with personal information, please contact us immediately at chaturangagame@gmail.com and we will take steps to delete that information promptly.

If you are between 13 and 17, we encourage you to review this policy with a parent or guardian before creating an account.

8. GDPR Rights (EU/UK Users)

If you are located in the European Union, European Economic Area, or the United Kingdom, you have the following rights under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) or UK GDPR:

  • Right to access: Request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Right to rectification: Request correction of inaccurate personal data.
  • Right to erasure ("right to be forgotten"): Request deletion of your personal data, subject to our legal obligations.
  • Right to restrict processing: Request that we limit how we use your data.
  • Right to data portability: Receive your personal data in a structured, machine-readable format.
  • Right to object: Object to processing of your data for direct marketing or based on our legitimate interests.

Our lawful basis for processing your personal data is our legitimate interest in providing and improving our service, and (for advertising) your consent where required. To exercise any of these rights, contact us at chaturangagame@gmail.com. We will respond within 30 days.

9. CCPA Rights (California Users)

If you are a California resident, the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) gives you the following rights:

  • Right to know: Request information about the categories and specific pieces of personal information we have collected about you.
  • Right to delete: Request deletion of your personal information, subject to certain exceptions.
  • Right to opt out of sale: We do not sell your personal information to third parties. However, Google AdSense may share your data with advertising partners — you may opt out via Google's Ads Settings linked in Section 4.
  • Right to non-discrimination: We will not discriminate against you for exercising your CCPA rights.

To submit a CCPA request, email us at chaturangagame@gmail.com with "CCPA Request" in the subject line.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will update the "Last Updated" date at the top of this page. Continued use of our website after changes are posted constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

For significant changes, we will make reasonable efforts to notify you — for example, via a notice on the website or (if you have an account) by email.

11. Contact

For any questions, concerns, or requests related to this Privacy Policy or your personal data, please contact us:

Email: chaturangagame@gmail.com

We take all privacy inquiries seriously and will respond within a reasonable timeframe.
