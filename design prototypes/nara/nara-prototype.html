Silhouette identifiers
Pagdi dome — spans 70% of viewBox width at base. Widest element on the piece, unmistakable at every size. The single fold ridge at 37px is what makes it a turban, not a ball.

Trishul tip — three-pronged spear tip in upper-left quadrant. At 32px renders as a Y-fork above the shaft line — immediately readable as "weapon."

Dhal circle — 12.5-radius circle at right edge is a second strong shape. Creates a spear↗ + shield○ two-shape silhouette rule that distinguishes Nara from every other piece at a glance.

Three-ridge test — at 44px, all three silhouette markers (dome / shaft diagonal / shield circle) are readable without any detail lines.
