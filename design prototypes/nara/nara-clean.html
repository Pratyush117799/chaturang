Nara — नर
पदाति  ·  Foot Soldier  ·  Chaturanga
Three SVG prototypes for the Nara piece — each a pure SVG circular disc with Indian decorative border ring and a hand-drawn Indian warrior silhouette. Inherits the disc system from the Ashwa; army colour fills both the decorative ring and the warrior figure.
Prototype 1 Dhanurdhari — धनुर्धारी
The Archer  ·  12-petal lotus ring  ·  Same border language as Ashwa
Foot soldier mid-draw — turban, armoured torso, wide archer's stance. The bow arc sweeping left and the draw arm pulling right create an unmistakable archer silhouette readable at 32 px. Lotus border is identical to the Ashwa so both pieces share visual language; they are distinguished by figure shape alone.

Integration

Drop-in replacement for the Ashwa symbol in renderBoardGrid(). The viewBox is identical (80×80). Replace <text>♞</text> with the Dhanurdhari <g> block; set its fill to playerColours[owner]. The petal fill and figure fill are the same value — one variable per army. Render at width="50" height="50" inside a 54 px square; all scaling is handled internally by the viewBox. At 32 px the bow-arc silhouette remains distinct from the Ashwa horse-head, providing two separate piece-type cues at extreme miniature sizes.
Prototype 2 Khadgadhari — खड्गधारी
Sword & Shield Warrior  ·  16-block meander ring  ·  Distinct border from Ashwa
A warrior mid-lunge — crested helmet with plume, khanda raised high in the right hand, round chakra shield on the left arm, wide aggressive stance. The segmented block border echoes running-key meander friezes in Mauryan stonework. Different border texture = different piece type — a secondary cue independent of figure shape.

Integration

The block ring uses 16 <rect> elements inside a single translate(40,40) group, each with a rotate(N) transform. Slightly more DOM than the 12-petal lotus but negligible at board scale. The wide chakra-shield circle makes this silhouette noticeably wider than P1 and P3, providing an extra size-based cue at 32–40 px. Resize identically: width="50" height="50" on a 54 px square.
Prototype 3 Vel Nara — वेल नर
Spear Carrier  ·  8 trishul-point motifs  ·  Strongest Indian iconographic signature
Marching infantry — turban, upright posture, right arm thrusting a vel diagonally forward and upward, left wrist bearing a small round shield. The Trishul-inspired border (8 triple-point motifs, each referencing Shiva's trident) is the most distinctly Indian decorative element of the three prototypes. The long diagonal spear creates a directional silhouette readable at 32 px.

Integration

The trishul border renders as 8 groups of 3 polygon elements — the lightest of the three borders. At 32 px the triple-points collapse to a clean radial dash ring, visually distinct from both the lotus and block borders. The diagonal spear silhouette is the simplest warrior shape: one dominant diagonal line anchored by a compact body. Recommended when a strong, unmistakably Indian thematic identity is the primary goal.
