Ratha — रथ
युद्ध रथ  ·  War Chariot  ·  Chaturanga
Three SVG prototypes for the Ratha piece — each on a light ivory inner disc with army-coloured fills and dark ink outlines on every element. Horse, chariot body, wheel spokes, charioteer and flag are visually separated at all sizes.
Prototype 1Dvibhuja Ratha — द्विभुज रथ
Two-horse war chariot  ·  Meander ring  ·  Charioteer + dhvaja flag
Two galloping horses, turbaned charioteer gripping reins, ornate chariot body with scroll volute, large 8-spoke chakra wheel, and dhvaja flag — all with dark outlines keeping every element distinct.

Integration

Drop-in for renderBoardGrid(). Same viewBox="0 0 80 80". The ivory inner disc (#f2e8d0) is baked in — only the border ring, shape fills, and outline colour are army-driven. Dark ink (#1a0d04) is applied as stroke on every path. Render at width="50" height="50" on a 54 px square.
Prototype 2Dhanurdhari Ratha — धनुर्धारी रथ
Archer's chariot  ·  16-petal lotus ring  ·  Drawn bow spanning the disc
Single powerful war horse, archer with bow fully drawn — bow arc sweeps far left while the string pulls right. Quiver on chariot side. The horizontal tension + round wheel creates an instantly legible silhouette. Dark outlines separate bow limbs, string, arrow shaft and chariot body.

The bow arc extends to x≈11 in viewBox space — unique crescent-over-wheel silhouette at 32 px. Bow and bowstring are stroked lines so they remain readable on any board square colour. Same width="50" height="50" rendering convention.
Prototype 3Chatri Ratha — छत्री रथ
Royal canopy chariot  ·  24-spoke Ashoka chakra ring  ·  Chatri dome + dhvaja
Two horses, ornate chatri parasol dome with visible ribs and scalloped valance fringe, dhvaja flag at the finial. The dome arch is the most architecturally distinct shape in the piece set. Dark rib lines and fringe are clearly visible against the ivory field.
